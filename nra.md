## How certification works (3 steps)

1. **Meet prerequisites** — 21+, U.S. citizen or legal resident, legally able to
   possess a firearm.
2. **Take the discipline's *basic student* course first** (instructor-led, NOT
   online) — e.g., **NRA Basics of Pistol Shooting** — pass the written exam and
   a shooting qualification. Bring that certificate to the instructor course.
3. **Take Basic Instructor Training (BIT) + the discipline Instructor course.**
   BIT is a ~6-hour block required once before any instructor course. Most
   Illinois providers below bundle BIT into the instructor weekend. You finish
   certified once the NRA Training Counselor endorses you.

Budget an extra **~$35–60** for the NRA credential activation fee (paid to NRA,
separate from the class).

When booking, confirm: (a) does this course include BIT, and (b) do I already
have the basic student-level cert in that discipline?

## 🦌 Rifle Instructor — path & classes

The path is the **same 3 steps** as above, with rifle-specific prerequisites:

1. **Prerequisites** — 21+, U.S. citizen/legal resident, legally able to possess
   a firearm (same as pistol).
2. **Take *NRA Basics of Rifle Shooting* (student course)** first — instructor-led,
   not online. Then pass the **rifle shooting qualification**: typically **24 of
   30 rounds in a 4½-inch group at 75 feet**, shot across **three positions**.
   Bring the student cert (90%+ written) to Day 1.
3. **BIT + Rifle Instructor course** — BIT (~6 hrs) is required once within the
   prior 24 months; the Illinois providers below include it as Day 1 of the
   instructor weekend. Finish certified once the Training Counselor endorses you.

> Note: BIT is shared across disciplines. If you do **pistol first** and earn BIT,
> you do **not** need to repeat BIT for rifle (as long as it's within 24 months) —
> ask the provider about a BIT-waived (rifle-only) rate.

## Note: Illinois Concealed Carry (CCL) instructor is separate

NRA instructor certification ≠ Illinois CCL instructor. To teach the IL 16-hour
CCL class you must separately apply through the **Illinois State Police**
(usually after becoming an NRA instructor): https://isp.illinois.gov/Foid/InstructorInfo
